EDUCATIONAL QUALITY GUIDE — illustrative project data, not a certified specification.

Dimensional quality
The sample training bracket has a nominal width of 50.00 mm and a tolerance of plus or minus 0.10 mm. Acceptable width is therefore 49.90 mm to 50.10 mm inclusive. Record results using a calibrated measuring instrument and identify rejected parts.

Document control
Use only the approved revision of a work instruction. Record the document identifier, revision and inspection date. If instructions conflict, stop the affected task and ask the responsible engineer for clarification.

Fault reporting
A maintenance record should include the asset identifier, date, observed symptoms, measured values, isolation status and corrective action. Do not invent missing measurements or fault causes.
