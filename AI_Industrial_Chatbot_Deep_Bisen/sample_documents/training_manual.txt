EDUCATIONAL SAMPLE MANUAL — invented training equipment, not a real machine.
Never use these example values for operating actual industrial equipment.

Bearing inspection
For the sample training conveyor, inspect bearings every 250 operating hours. Stop and isolate the conveyor before inspection. Record bearing noise, vibration and temperature trends. Escalate unusual changes to the maintenance supervisor.

Lubrication
The sample training conveyor uses manufacturer-approved grease. Clean the grease fitting before lubrication. The educational inspection interval is 250 operating hours. Do not infer a grease grade or quantity: those values are not specified in this document.

Motor overheating
For a motor overheating complaint on the training conveyor, inspect ventilation blockage and compare motor current with its nameplate after following approved isolation procedures. An authorized technician must investigate abnormal current. Do not bypass overload protection.

Energy isolation
Before maintenance, stop equipment, isolate every energy source, apply the approved lockout/tagout procedure, release stored energy and verify isolation. Only authorized personnel may perform this work. Consult the actual site procedure for a real machine.
